Getting started

Open the console and enter your email address — press Email me a code and a 6-digit sign-in code arrives in your inbox. Type it in and you're in. Codes last 10 minutes and work once; no password to remember. Prefer a password? Use the "Use a password instead" link — if your account came with a temporary password, that path makes you set your own (10+ characters) the first time. You can set or change a password any time from My profile.

Set up your profile

  • My profile → Display name: this is your code name — the only name callers ever see. Use the same one every time, per the {{manual}}. Your real name never reaches a caller.
  • Authenticator app (TOTP): strongly recommended. My profile → "Set up authenticator" shows a secret to add to Google Authenticator or similar; enter the 6-digit code to confirm. From then on, login asks for a code too.
  • Phone: supervisors' numbers power the tap-to-call "On call" chip in the console header.

Your status

The three buttons top-right of the console are how the system knows who is available:

  • On shift — you're on the line; waiting callers appear in your queue.
  • Backup (on call) — you're not on the line, but if a caller waits too long you'll be paged (in the console, and in Google Chat if the team webhook is set up).
  • Off — no pages, no queue.

Your shift & the schedule

The Schedule page is the month at a glance: red = unstaffed, amber = assigned but not yet confirmed, green = confirmed, dashed = someone needs cover. 👁 marks On Call Supervisor cover — the day and night blocks on the days they run{{#text.supervisionHours}} ({{text.supervisionHours}}){{/text.supervisionHours}}, created with the month and assignable only to supervisors and coordinators. A shift can carry more than one person: 🎓 marks an observation shift (OS) and 🤝 a supervised shift (SS) — a new volunteer sitting in beside the worker — numbered per person automatically (OS3 is their third observation). Hover over any slot for its hours. Staff click a slot to assign it, add a note, change that day's hours, add someone else to the shift, request cover or cancel it; coordinators change the OCS weekly pattern under Supervisor hours.

Submitting availability

  1. Open the month you're submitting for with the ‹ › arrows.
  2. Tick the shifts you can work — and under each ticked shift, untap any weekdays you can't do it on (all days are on by default). Say how many shifts you'd like, and use the "Only, Nots and Also" box for one-off specifics (e.g. NOT the 3rd Monday).
  3. Submit every month, even if you're away — the manual's rule, so the scheduler never has to chase. Resubmitting replaces your earlier answer.

Confirming and swapping

  • Your assigned shifts appear under My shifts — press Confirm when you're sure you can make it (aim for 72–24 hours before, per the manual).
  • Can't make a shift? Press "Can't make it". The shift shows as cover wanted to the whole team and staff are notified — but it stays yours until a colleague presses Take it. Finding a replacement remains your responsibility — though for last-minute gaps a supervisor can press Email everyone to send a ready-made (and editable) cover request to everyone who could take the shift, each person getting their own copy.
  • Calendar feed gives you a private URL — subscribe from Google Calendar ("Add other calendars → From URL") and your {{shortName}} shifts appear in your own calendar. Don't share the URL; it's yours.

At the start of your shift

The console front page is built around the manual's checklist: the Active directives panel (read it every shift), your unread Noticeboard badge (the notebooks), and any feedback from your supervisor waiting at the top right.

Support workers

Taking a chat

  1. Set yourself On shift. Waiting callers appear on the left with their pre-chat concern and wait time. A red risk indicated badge means the pre-chat survey flagged suicidal thoughts or recent self-harm — these callers jump the queue.
  2. Press Accept. The caller is told they're connected to your code name; their whole pre-chat survey is in the caller record on the right.
  3. Type / in the message box for canned responses — including the manual's qualification scripts ("Qualifications", "Supervision"), the safety check, and the emergency numbers. The link at the bottom of that list opens the Canned responses page: add your own there. Supervisors and coordinators look after the shared ones (coordinators also find it under Admin, with a one-click starter set when there are none).
  4. Ask for help pings every supervisor and backup, with your note — use it any time, mid-chat or not. You are not alone.
  5. End chat when the conversation closes; then finish and submit the intake. If the caller disappears, the system notices within about a minute and tells you — you can still submit the intake.

Trainees: your console is read-only. When a worker or supervisor invites you to observe, the live chat appears on your screen; the caller is never told.

Support workers

Writing the intake

Every contact gets an intake. The form auto-saves as you type — a crash or refresh loses nothing — and much of it is pre-filled from the caller's own pre-chat answers, so you correct rather than retype. Times, duration, queue wait and the transcript are recorded automatically. The badge beside Intake shows "draft saved" with the time; if it turns red ("not saved — retrying") your connection dropped and it keeps trying. Submitting shows a green confirmation at the bottom of the screen.

  • Phone calls: press Start phone intake under My chat. You get the same intake with nothing pre-filled (phone callers have no pre-chat survey), plus referrals and follow-ups; submitting it ends the call. Started one by mistake? Discard at the top throws it away.
  • The Mandatory Suicide Question works as a ladder: answer Ideation, and Intention / Plan / Means only appear as you go — rungs you never reach are never recorded as "asked".
  • "User's feelings and brief content" is about the service user; "Process of the conversation" is about you and what you did — skills used, what worked, what you checked.
  • "Would you like your supervisor to follow up with you?" — answering yes opens an item in the supervisor review queue. Use it after any conversation that stays with you.
  • Highlight this contact puts it on the wallboard so other workers are aware of the incident.

Respect the anonymity of callers — never include names in an intake. The reminder above the form is the manual's rule.

Support workers

The caller record

The right-hand column shows everything known about the caller: their pre-chat answers, how many times they've contacted us today and before, any directives (colour-coded info / caution / alert — read them before engaging), and previous referrals. Drag the divider between the chat and the record to make either side bigger; your width is remembered.

Support workers

Follow-ups & referrals

  • Referrals: search the resource directory from inside the chat and attach what you refer. Previous referrals to the same caller show automatically. Missing a resource? Suggest it — a coordinator approves it into the directory.
  • Schedule follow-up creates a dated reminder attached to the caller; your open follow-ups sit on the console's left column, marked red when overdue, with a Done button.
Supervisors

Review, feedback & the wallboard

  • Review lists workers who asked for follow-up first, then recent submitted intakes (⚑ marks highlighted contacts). Open one to see the intake beside the transcript, and send feedback — the worker sees it at their next login and acknowledges it. Sending feedback marks the intake reviewed. Opening a contact here is recorded in the audit log.
  • Directives: add one from any active chat's caller record (Add directive) with a level — info, caution, or alert. Retire it when it no longer applies; it stays in the record.
  • Wallboard: live queue, workers on shift, open help requests, highlighted contacts, and every next-24h shift that is unstaffed or unconfirmed — the manual's end-of-shift check, always on.
  • Reports: pick a date range for outcome counts, wait times, and per-question breakdowns, with CSV download. Aggregates only — no content or names ever appear in reports.
  • On the noticeboard, supervisors create and pin topics.
Coordinators

Admin, forms & operations

  • Admin → people: add workers one at a time or CSV-import{{#legacy.system}} from {{legacy.system}}'s Vols–Staff export{{/legacy.system}}. Each new account gets a temporary password; roles, service status, supervisor links and password resets all live here. Every action is audit-logged.
  • Admin → Intake form options: edit the choices of every question on the worker intake and the caller pre-chat survey — add, rename, reorder, retire. Each save publishes a new version instantly; past records keep the wording they were written under. Nothing here needs a developer.
  • Resources: approve suggested resources, edit and retire directory entries, or import from a spreadsheet — save it as CSV with at least a Name column (plus any of Category, Description, Website, Phone, Languages), preview what each row will do, then import. Rows already in the directory are skipped unless you tick "update".
  • Schedule: "Create month's slots" builds the empty month (coordinators only), with as many seats per shift as People per shift says — raise a shift to 2 for a phone line and a chat line, then press "Create month's slots" again to add the seats to a month already built (lowering it never removes anyone); supervisors and coordinators can click any slot to assign (workers available for that shift on that weekday are marked ✓), add supervisor/observer slots, set notes, or request cover. The roster shows staff everyone in service — who has and hasn't submitted, with a filter box, each name a click-to-email link, and a one-click reminder to exactly the people missing. Coordinators additionally get Auto-fill from availability — a reviewed draft that fills every open slot from what people submitted (shifts, weekdays and how many they asked for — at most 4 for anyone who left that blank). It shares shifts out evenly, never gives one person two shifts on a day or shifts whose hours overlap, and shows how the draft is spread before you apply it. It does not read "Only, Nots and Also", so check those by hand — and Recent changes, the latest schedule actions with a link to the full log in its own window (search, filter by person or kind of change, download as CSV), availability submissions and cover emails included.
  • Ops (ops.html): health checks, which settings are configured, the last 24h of events, and recent errors — plus a button that sends test notifications to the Google Chat webhooks.

Google Chat notifications

  1. In the team's Google Chat space: space name → Apps & integrations → Webhooks → Add. Copy the webhook URL.
  2. Add it as TL_NOTIFY_WEBHOOK_URL in Vercel (and optionally a second space as TL_OPS_WEBHOOK_URL for ops alerts), set TL_BASE_URL to the app's address, and redeploy.
  3. Press Send test notifications on the Ops page — both channels should receive a hello.

Troubleshooting

Something isn't working, or you'd like something changed

Press Report a problem at the foot of the menu, on any page. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened — the page you're on and your browser are attached for you, so please leave out any caller details. You get a reference like TL-0007 and an email whenever it's updated, and My problem reports shows everything you've sent and what's been done. If something marked fixed still isn't right, open it there and press Still not fixed. Coordinators work through reports under Problems & requests on the Ops page. While a report is open you can add a comment to it, or close it yourself if it's sorted.

I can't log in

First try Email me a code — it works whenever you can read your email, whatever state your password is in. If no code arrives: check spam, check the email is spelled as registered, and wait a minute (three codes per address per quarter-hour). Otherwise: password spelled right (a coordinator can reset it — you'll get a new temporary one); your authenticator code if you enabled TOTP (a password reset also clears TOTP); too many attempts pauses login for 15 minutes; and accounts marked "left" cannot log in.

I lost my authenticator

Ask a coordinator to reset your password — that clears TOTP too. Log in with an emailed code or the temporary password, then re-enrol the authenticator from My profile.

The chat looks frozen

The console reconnects by itself and nothing is lost — messages you send while offline show as pending and deliver when the connection returns. If it persists, refresh the page: your chat, intake draft and everything else come straight back.

A caller says the chat won't load

Ask them to try a different browser or network, and offer the phone line. If several callers report it, tell a coordinator — the Ops page and /healthz show whether the system itself is unwell.

I'm not getting notifications

In-console banners and pages need the console open in a tab (allow browser notifications when asked). Google Chat messages need the webhook configured — a coordinator can verify with the Ops page's test button.

Something else is wrong

Post in the Workers' Notebook or use Ask for help. For system problems, coordinators check the Ops page first, then the platform logs.
